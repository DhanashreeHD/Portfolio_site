GAME DESIGNER

My name is Dhanashree, an upcoming Game Designer.

I’m a Game Designer with an MSc in Computer Games from Queen Mary University of London who blends creative exploration with structured problem-solving. I enjoy shaping mechanics around player behavior, refining systems through iteration, and making thoughtful trade-offs when constraints demand it. I’ve built projects across Unity, Unreal, and custom C++ architectures, working with C#, Blender, and gameplay systems from prototype to polish. My experience at Tata Consultancy Services strengthened my ability to debug under pressure, think structurally, and collaborate in fast-paced environments—skills that inform how I design and build games today.
